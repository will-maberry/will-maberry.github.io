USDA ARS + MISSISSIPPI STATE / JULY 2025 – CURRENT

One-Month-Ahead HPAI Early-Warning Forecasting

I developed a leakage-safe forecasting framework to test whether county-level HPAI early-warning signal remains detectable when forecasts are restricted to information strictly available at the time of prediction.

The framework uses a 24-month rolling-origin simulation across the conterminous United States. At each forecast origin, future information is excluded, unavailable forecast-month covariates are synthesized from historical data, models and alert thresholds are developed using prior information, and predictions are evaluated one monthly rollover.

The evaluation spans four modeling families: logistic regression, tabular ensembles, recurrent-only models, and graph-temporal models. Feature-family sensitivity and retraining analyses examine which information sources contribute to retained predictive signal.

Forecast outputs are integrated into an interactive web application dashboard, translating modeling outputs into interpretable biosurveillance risk tiers.

SCALE: 74,616 county-month forecasts / 24 forecast origins
PREVALENCE: 563 outbreak-positive county-months / 0.8%
DEPLOYABLE: 72.8–73.8% balanced accuracy / 72.7–74.9% recall

Ongoing research · manuscript in preparation.

USDA ARS / MAY – JULY 2025

HPAI Risk Classification & Pseudo-Forecasting

My computational epidemiology research began during a USDA ARS internship, where I developed national county-month risk models integrating environmental, agricultural, geospatial, and migratory-bird data. Rare-event classification achieved 75%+ balanced accuracy on 2022–2024 surveillance data with approximately 1% outbreak-positive observations.

I then evaluated pseudo-forecasting under temporal shift using observed future climate covariates. This work motivated the central question of my subsequent research: can early-warning signal be retained when forecasts are restricted strictly to information available at prediction time?

I presented this research to USDA ARS leadership and multiple National Program Leaders. The project later received the Judge's Choice Award at UTA College of Engineering's Innovation Day 2026.
